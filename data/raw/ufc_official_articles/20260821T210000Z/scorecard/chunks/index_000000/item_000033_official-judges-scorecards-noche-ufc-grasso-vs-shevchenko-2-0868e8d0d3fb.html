See How The Judges Scored Every Round Of Noche UFC: Grasso vs Shevchenko 2, Live From T-Mobile Arena In Las Vegas
Sep. 16, 2023

Noche UFC was an incredible experience.

T-Mobile Arena was packed with passionate fans from the outset, and the athletes that made the walk to the Octagon gave them plenty to cheer about throughout, with several incredible finishes and a number of competitive battles, culminating in an instant classic between Alexa Grasso and Valentina Shevchenko.

Here's how the judges scored all the action. | Results, Highlights & More

Noche UFC Scorecards 

Josefine Knutsson vs Marnic Mann 

Josefine Knutsson (30-24, 30-25, 30-27) defeats Marnic Mann by unanimous decision | Results, Highlights & More

 

Alex Reyes vs Charlie Campbell 

Charlie Campbell defeats Alex Reyes by TKO (right hand) at 3:38 of Round 1 | Results, Highlights & More

 

Tracy Cortez vs Jasmine Jasudavicius 

Tracy Cortez (30-27, 30-27, 29-28) defeats Jasmine Jasudavicius by unanimous decision | Results, Highlights & More 

 

Edgar Chairez vs Daniel Lacerda

Edgar Chairez versus Daniel Lacerda declared a no decision at 3:47 of Round 1 | Results, Highlights & More 

 

Roman Kopylov vs Josh Fremd 

Roman Kopylov defeats Josh Fremd by TKO (body shot) at 4:44 of Round 2 | Results, Highlights & More

 

Loopy Godinez vs Elise Reed

Loopy Godinez defeats Elise Reed by submission, rear naked choke, Round 2, 3:38 | Results, Highlights & More 

 

Fernando Padilla vs Kyle Nelson 

Kyle Nelson (29-28, 29-28, 30-27) defeats Fernando Padilla by unanimous decision | Results, Highlights & More

 

Daniel Zellhuber vs Christos Giagos 

Daniel Zellhuber defeats Christos Giagos by submission, anaconda choke, Round 2, 3:26 | Results, Highlights & More

 

Raul Rosas Jr. vs Terrence Mitchell 

Raul Rosas Jr defeats Terrance Mitchell by TKO, Round 1, :54 | Results, Highlights & More 

 

Co-Main Event: Kevin Holland vs Jack Della Maddalena 

Jack Della Maddalena (29-28, 29-28) defeats Kevin Holland (29-28) by split decision | Results, Highlights & More

 

Main Event: Alexa Grasso vs Valentina Shevchenko

Alexa Grasso (48-47) and Valentina Shevchenko (48-47) fight to a split draw (47-47) | Results, Highlights & More
