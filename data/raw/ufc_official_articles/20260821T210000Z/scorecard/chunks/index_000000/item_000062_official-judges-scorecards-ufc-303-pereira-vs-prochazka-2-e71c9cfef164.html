Official Scorecards | UFC 303: Pereira vs Procházka 2

See How The Judges Scored Every Round Of UFC 303: Pereira vs Procházka 2, Live From T-Mobile Arena In Las Vegas
Jun. 30, 2024

What a night!

The annual International Fight Week pay-per-view always has a way of delivering and on Saturday night at T-Mobile Arena, that held true.

Opening with a competitive clash of styles in the welterweight division and a critical battle in the women’s bantamweight ranks, the evening progressed onto a short-notice battle between light heavyweights before Diego Lopes and Dan Ige showed out and Alex Pereira closed out the show as only “Poatan” can. | Prelim Results | Main Card Results
