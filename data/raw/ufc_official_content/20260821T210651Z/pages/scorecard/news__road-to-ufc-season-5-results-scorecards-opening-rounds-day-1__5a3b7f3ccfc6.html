Road To UFC Results | Season 5 Opening Rounds: Day 1

See The Fight Results And Official Scorecards From The Opening Round Of Road To UFC Season 5, Live From Galaxy Arena In Macau On Thursday, May 28
By E. Spencer Kyte, On X: @SpencerKyte • May. 28, 2026

Road To UFC kicked off Season 5 in spectacular fashion on Thursday, launching the bantamweight and featherweight tournaments inside Galaxy Arena.

In the featured UFC lightweight main event, Rongzhu needed just over a minute to stop Victor Martinez and secure his third consecutive victory. The co-headliner saw equal dominance, with Xie Bin making quick work of Yudi Cahyadi in their non-tournament featherweight clash.

Read all about how the action unfolded with our fight-by-fight recap of the entire night below.

Results, Fight Recaps & Scorecards:

Rongzhu defeated Victor Martinez by TKO (strikes) at 1:10 of Round 1

Rongzhu wasted no time dispatching Victor Martinez to register his third straight win, cutting him down with calf kicks before driving home a right hand that ended the fight.

The Chinese veteran took Martinez off his feet with the second heavy low kick he landed, and once he rose to his feet, Rongzhu fired off a laser-sighted thunder bolt that dropped Martinez. As he looked to swarm for the finish, referee Marc Goddard intervened, halting the action.

Just a brilliant effort from the 26-year-old, who has looked outstanding since joining forces with the team at City Kickboxing. He called for a matchup with surging Australian Quillan Salkilld, whom he was scheduled to fight earlier this year before being forced out with an injury, following the victory.

Xie Bin defeated Yudi Cahyadi by TKO (right hand) at 2:05 of Round 1

Xie Bin made a statement in the night’s non-tournament featherweight contest, driving him a long right hand that stopped Indonesian veteran Yudi Cahyadi before the midway point of the opening round.

Xie Bin punches Yudi Cahyadi in their non-tournament featherweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

Not long after the broadcast team of John Gooden and Alan Jouban discussed the slow starts and prior setbacks the Chinese veteran has experienced in previous RTU and Dana White’s Contender Series appearances, Xie unleashed a right hand that caught Cahyadi flush and forced him to cover up. As Xie came forward, Cahyadi collapsed to the floor, ending the fight.

This was a beautiful finish by the 28-year-old, who landed on the wrong side of a split decision in the Season 3 finale opposite Zhu Kangjie last May. Xie has shown flashes of upside through his various appearances in front of the UFC brass, and this just might have been enough to finally earn him the call to compete inside the Octagon.

George Mangos defeated Yuito Yanagawa by TKO (knee to liver) at 3:19

George Mangos had to navigate a deep submission attempt, but the Australian prospect made good on his prediction to get it done in one, finishing Yuito Yanagawa with a nasty knee to the liver in the first round.

George Mangos reacts after his TKO win over Yuito Yanagawa in their featherweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

The 22-year-old was given a scare in the first half of the contest, getting stuck in a tight arm-in guillotine choke in open space that took patience and poise to escape. But once they clambered back to their feet, Mangos tickled Yanagawa’s midsection with a left knee along the fence and then dropped him with a second, heavier knee to the liver.

After faltering last year on Dana White’s Contender Series, the Lion’s Den Academy product collected his second straight win while maintaining his 100 percent finishing rate. Mangos will now face Ailinuer in the semifinals in Shanghai later this year, with a spot in the finals on the line.

Aheijiang Ailinuer defeated GwanWoo Lim by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)

Aheijiang Ailinuer showed his superior grappling acumen in the penultimate featherweight tournament pairing of the day, grinding out a hard-fought decision win over South Korea’s GwanWoo Lim.

Aheijiang Ailinuer reacts after his unanimous decision win over GwanWoo in their featherweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

The Chinese hopeful did well to close the distance and neutralize Lim’s considerable size advantage, wrestling him to the ground repeatedly and transitioning quickly when the two landed in scrambles. Though he was unable to find a finish, this was a clear and decisive victory for the 27-year-old, who was never in any real danger at any point.

After missing out on the opportunity to compete last season due to injury, this was a tremendous start to Season 5 for Ailinuer, who scored his fourth straight win with the clear decision on Saturday in Macau.

Kasib Murdoch defeated Yunosuke Minami by submission (arm-triangle choke) at 3:46 of Round 3

Kasib Murdoch claimed victory in the clash of undefeated bantamweights that wrapped the opening round in the 135-pound tournament, collecting a third-round submission win over Yunosuke Minami.

Kasib Murdoch punches Yunosuke Minami in a bantamweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

While Minami was the faster, most dangerous of the two in space, Murdoch was able to close the distance and dominate with his wrestling, logging more control time and piling up increasing amounts of damage as the fight progressed. Minami was docked a point in the second round for grabbing the fence, but it was inconsequential, as the 25-year-old New Zealander forced the tap in the late stages of the fight.

his turned into a one-sided beatdown once Murdoch recognized he was able to get inside, get the fight to the canvas, and keep Minami there. One of the clear favorites heading into the competition, Murdoch moved one step closer to claiming a spot on the roster with his outstanding effort here, setting up a clash with Rabindra Dhant in the next round.

Dagiisuren Chagnaadorj defeated Regezhen by split decision (29-28, 28-29, 29-28)

The second matchup in the featherweight tournament played out as a tale of three distinct rounds, with Dagiisuren Chagnaadorj earning a hard-earned win over China’s Regezhen.

Dagiisuren Chagnaadorj punches Regezhen in a featherweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

Mongolia’s Chagnaadorj did well to lean on his grappling in the opening stanza, avoiding the big swings from Regezhen while dragging the fight to the canvas and controlling things there for stretches in the competitive first five minutes. Regezhen found his rhythm in the second as Chagnaadorj seemed to be slowing, seemingly drawing level before Chagnaadorj was the more active and effective of the two down the stretch.

For the second time in the evening, the judges were called upon to render a verdict, with Chagnaadorj coming away on the happy side of the split decision verdict and advancing to the semifinals. That’s now three straight wins for the 26-year-old Mongolian fighter, who will meet South Korea’s YoungJae Song in the next round.

Rabindra Dhant defeated Kimbert Alintozon by TKO (strikes) at 4:37 of Round 2

Rabindra Dhant gave the small, but vocal contingent of Nepalese fans in attendance in Macau plenty to cheer about, delivering a well-rounded performance while securing a second-round stoppage win over Kimbert Alintozon.

Rabindra Dhant kicks Kimbert Alintozon in a bantamweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

Dhant showed the full spectrum of his skills in booking his place in the semifinals. In the second round, it was his grappling that put him in a position to advance, as Dhant was able to drag Alintozon to the canvas and float into mount, posture up and eventually pound out the finish, showing tremendous positional control and keen finishing instincts.

Just a brilliant effort for the first fighter from Nepal to compete on Road to UFC. Dhant certainly had a big following in attendance and undoubtedly earned legions of more supporters with his performance here.

Ryo Tajima defeated Ti Haitao by split decision (29-28, 28-29, 29-28)

Bantamweights Ryo Tajima and Ti Haitao had the crowd at Galaxy Arena on the edge of their seats from start to finish as they battled back-and-forth for a place in the semifinals.

Ryo Tajima punches Ti Haitao in a bantamweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

This one was all-out action throughout, with Tajima and Ti becoming the first pair of fighters to advance into the third round on the evening. Each man had moments of success in each phase, with Tajima mixing in timely takedowns while hang with Ti on the feet, while the Chinese athlete chopped away at his Japanese foe’s lead leg with punishing low kicks.

The judges were called upon to determine a victor and when the scorecards were collected and totalled, it was Tajima that emerged on the happy side of the split decision verdict, much to the dismay of the partisan crowd. This was a competitive and entertaining fight, setting up a semifinal matchup between Tajima and Chungren Koren later this year.

YoungJae Song defeated Jin Aoi by submission (rear-naked choke) at 3:39 of Round 2

YoungJae Song put himself behind the eight ball with a point deduction in the second round but made sure to secure his place in the semifinals by collecting a submission win over Jin Aoi.

YoungJae Song submits Jin Aoi in a featherweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

The South Korean featherweight, who competed on Season 3 of Road to UFC, seemed a little unfocused at points in this opening round contest, as he was warned for a fence grab in the first and docked a point for kneeing Aoi when he was grounded in the second. But as the two battled in the clinch late in the second, Song pulled Aoi away from the fence and straight into a back-mount position, immediately lacing up the rear-naked choke and securing the finish.

Just when it seemed like the point deduction was sure to cost “Super Rookie,” he found a way to maintain his 100 percent finishing rate and claim a spot in the next round.

Chungreng Koren defeated Ryuho Miyaguchi by submission (rear-naked choke) at 4:00 of Round 2

Chungreng Koren opened the fifth season of Road to UFC with a relentless finish of Ryuho Miyaguchi, finally halting things with a submission late in the second round.

Chungreng Koren submits Ryuho Miyaguchi in a bantamweight fight during the Opening Round of Road To UFC Season 5 in Macau on May 28, 2026. (Jeffrey Bottari/Zuffa LLC)

After a competitive first round, Miyaguchi looked to be finding his range in the early moments of the second, tagging Choren with several straight right hands. But the Indian fighter stood in and dropped Miyaguchi with a lunging left hand, kicking off an extended stretch where Choren looked to halt things with strikes before switching off to the submission and securing the tap.

This was a gutsy effort from “The Indian Rhino,” who accepted this fight on short notice and made the most of it. Unfortunately, Choren missed weight on Wednesday, otherwise this would have been a perfect night for the emotional 28-year-old as he advanced to the semifinals.
