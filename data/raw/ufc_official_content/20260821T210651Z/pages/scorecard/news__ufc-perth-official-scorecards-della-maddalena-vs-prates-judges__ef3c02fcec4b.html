Official Scorecards | UFC Perth

See How The Judges Score Every Round Of Every Fight From UFC Fight Night: Della Maddalena vs Prates, Live From RAC Arena In Perth, Western Australia On May 2, 2026
By UFC Staff Report • May. 2, 2026

The Octagon returned to Perth, Western Australia, headlined by a thrilling welterweight showdown that saw Carlos Prates stop former champion Jack Della Maddalena in the third round. The stacked card featured 12 of Australia’s top talents, including Perth’s own Quillan Salkilld, who scored a first-round knockout of Beneil Dariush in Saturday’s co-main event.

See how the judges scored every round of every fight below. For more UFC Perth coverage, see Main Card Results and Prelim Results

Official Scorecards:

Dom Mar Fan vs Kody Steele

Official Result: Kody Steele defeated Dom Mar Fan by submission (heel hook) at 3:56 of Round 1

Jonathan Micallef vs Themba Gorimbo

Official Result: Jonathan Micallef defeated Themba Gorimbo by split decision (28-29, 29-28, 29-28)

Ben Johnston vs Wes Schultz

Official Result: Wes Schultz defeated Ben Johnston by submission (guillotine choke) at 1:50 of Round 3

Colby Thicknesse vs Vince Morales

Official Result: Colby Thicknesse defeated Vince Morales by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Jacob Malkoun vs Gerald Meerschaert

Official Result: Jacob Malkoun defeated Gerald Meerschaert by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Junior Tafa vs Kevin Christian

Official Result: Junior Tafa defeated Kevin Christian by KO (elbows) at 2:42 of Round 1

Cam Rowston vs Robert Bryczek

Official Result: Cam Rowston defeated Robert Bryczek by unanimous decision (30-27, 30-27, 30-27)

Tai Tuivasa vs Louie Sutherland

Official Result: Louie Sutherland defeated Tai Tuivasa by unanimous decision (30-26, 30-26, 30-26)

Shamil Gaziev vs Brando Peričić

Official Result: Brando Peričić defeated Shamil Gaziev by KO (strikes) at 3:44 of Round 2

Marwan Rahiki vs Ollie Schmid

Official Result: Marwan Rahiki defeated Ollie Schmid by TKO (strikes) at 2:47 of Round 1

Tim Elliott vs Steve Erceg

Official Result: Steve Erceg defeated Tim Elliott by unanimous decision (29-28, 29-28, 29-28)

Beneil Dariush vs Quillan Salkilld

Official Result: Quillan Salkilld defeated Beneil Dariush by TKO (strikes) at 3:29 of Round 1

Main Event: Jack Della Maddalena vs Carlos Prates

Official Result: Carlos Prates defeated Jack Della Maddalena by TKO (strikes) at 3:17 of Round 3

UFC Fight Night: Della Maddalena vs Prates took place live from RAC Arena in Perth, Western Australia on May 2, 2026. See the final Prelim & Main Card Results, Official Scorecards and Who Won Bonuses - and relive the action on UFC FIGHT PASS!
