Official Scorecards | UFC 316: Dvalishvili vs O'Malley 2

See How The Judges Scored Every Round Of UFC 316: Dvalishvili vs O'Malley 2, Live From Prudential Center In Newark On June 7
By UFC Staff Report • Jun. 8, 2025

UFC returned to Newark, New Jersey with another stacked card, headlined by a pair of blockbuster championship bouts.

In the main event, bantamweight champion Merab Dvalishvili submitted No. 1 ranked contender Sean O’Malley in their highly anticipated rematch. The co-main event saw Kayla Harrison submit Julianna Peña in the third round to become the new women's bantamweight champion. Here's how the judges scored every round of every fight:
