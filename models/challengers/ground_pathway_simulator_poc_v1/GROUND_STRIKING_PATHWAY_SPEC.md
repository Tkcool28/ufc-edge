# Competing KO pathway
Ground KO location is unobserved. At training boundary only, attribute each KO winner a fractional latent ground KO equal to ground significant attempts / total significant attempts in that winning bout (complete source records; total>0). This equal-per-attempt attribution is an assumption, not measured ground KO. Division pooled ground KO/ground attempts shrinks with 1000 ground attempts toward global. Division ground KO fraction shrinks with20 KO wins toward global. Ground hazard=directional conditional GnP activity * pooled ground conversion. Sparse fighter-specific ground conversion is not attempted. Save exact latent numerator/denominator and complete-attribution bout count.

Standing KO uses geometric KO win/min versus opponent KO loss/min, multiplied by sqrt(geometric KD creation/allowed rate / population geometric KD rate), and (1-pooled latent ground KO fraction)/(1-population decision-control share). This preserves existing striking creation/vulnerability concepts independently of GnP. It is a non-ground KO approximation; KO records and KD locations do not identify true standing finish hazard. No direct inference that every ground strike can KO.
