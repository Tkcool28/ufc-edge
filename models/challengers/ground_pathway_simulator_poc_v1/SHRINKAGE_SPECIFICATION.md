# Frozen shrinkage
Fold population pools: completed governed UFC histories in [2015-01-01, Jan1 outer year). No post-boundary population updating. Division means partially pool toward global with 300 exposure minutes for duration-rate means and control, 100 attempts for TD/SUB conversion means. No outcome-selected constants.

Count-rate Gamma-Poisson posterior mean (n+k*mu)/(d+k): k=30 elapsed minutes ACCESS/SUB and their rate counterparts, k=60 GnP and allowed GnP, 30 KD created/allowed, 60 KO created/allowed. These rates use correlated bout aggregates; no exact credible-interval claims. Generic control/allowed control uses exposure-weighted pseudo-prior k=30 minutes, NOT independent Bernoulli seconds. TD conversion/resistance uses Beta-style mean k=20 attempts. SUB conversion/allowed uses Beta-style mean k=50 coherent recorded attempts. Effective support d/(d+k), never discard tiny denominators. 1/1 SUB conversion remains dominated by 50 prior attempts. Missing numerator/denominator pair contributes neither; genuine measured zero counts do contribute. Population priors are training-only; fighter history updates strictly before each target event date.
