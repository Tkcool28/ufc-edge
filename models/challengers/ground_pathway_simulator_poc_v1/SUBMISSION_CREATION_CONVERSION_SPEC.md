# Submission creation / conversion
Creation follows directional, shrunk work-rate versus allowed-work-rate, divided by the explicit control proxy. Conversion is sqrt(A shrunk SUB success/attempt * B shrunk SUB loss/attempts faced). For each source bout require complete attempts and attempts>=submission success (0 or1). Zero-attempt SUB wins are excluded from conversion pairs only and counted, never repaired to a made-up attempt; creation history remains unchanged. Recorded attempts are not verified independent threat trials. Beta-style pooling is a conservative proxy; pooled-conversion ablation substitutes division priors. No submission-activity multiplier or outcome-fitted constant.
