# IMPLICATIONS FOR HYBRID SIMULATOR

Structural implications only; no simulator code, transitions, hazards or parameter estimates are created.

1. **Method pathways should remain distinct.** Governed KD/damage and KO-history/vulnerability environments are KO-oriented in historical outcomes; takedown-access/submission-pressure and pressure/attempts-faced environments are submission-enriched. A generic finish propensity discards important method composition.
2. **Grappling is not synonymous with submission.** B3 still has 27.0% KO/TKO across all qualifying bouts, alongside 27.9% submission. Dominant grappling may coexist with multiple terminal methods. This is consistent with the Sacramento postmortem's warning against deleting the TKO branch from a grappler's method tree, but the aggregate audit cannot identify ground-and-pound mechanisms or which fighter delivered the finish.
3. **Division-dependent baseline mix and scheduled duration are separate contexts.** Heavyweight and Light Heavyweight remain KO-heavy in three-round fights; Flyweight and several women's divisions have a different conditional mix. Five-round exposure is selected title/main-event context and does not have a universal causal effect.
4. **Decision/survival environments matter.** Women's Strawweight/Flyweight show substantial decision prevalence. Some defensive/low-pressure panels are also decision-heavy, but D1 is composition-sensitive with only 31 finishes, and D3 era samples are modest. Do not define deterministic survival states from those percentages.
5. **Occupancy, access, threat, conversion and outcome must remain distinct.** Submission attempts faced is not direct submission survival skill. Successful takedowns do not establish exact position, and KO_TKO does not distinguish standing from ground finishes. The audit does not observe within-fight state order or transitions.

The earlier feasibility research supports round-resolution observable outputs and a competing-method architecture as a possible future research direction, with explicit limits on finer transition assumptions. This audit adds a reusable method-composition reference for checking a future simulator's aggregated outputs. It provides neither a time-conditioned risk set nor a causal hazard: 47.6% Heavyweight KO is a bout proportion, not a per-second/per-round transition rate.

Any future hybrid must estimate and validate round dynamics from separately governed longitudinal observations, handle survival/selection, and test coherent winner × method × time outputs. Do not turn descriptive rates or archetype differences into transition probabilities, damage variables, or literal fatigue. A model-independent reference can be consulted without automatically becoming a feature or simulator parameter table.
