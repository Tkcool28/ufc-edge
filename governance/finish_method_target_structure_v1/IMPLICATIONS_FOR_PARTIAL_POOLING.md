# IMPLICATIONS FOR PARTIAL POOLING

## Was the MOV0 hierarchy simply aimed at the wrong target?

**The audit cannot establish that. It does show a different group structure worth testing at the conditional method target.** MOV0 FINISH-vs-DECISION and KO-vs-SUB among finishes are different questions. Division affects both descriptively; it would be inaccurate to say it only weakly affects generic finish everywhere.

For example, Heavyweight is 61.0% finish versus 49.9% UFC-wide (+11.1 pp), and 78.1% KO conditional on finish versus 64.2% (+13.9 pp). Light Heavyweight is 64.1% finish and 73.3% conditional KO. Flyweight is 45.0% finish but 50.7% conditional KO. Women's Strawweight is 33.6% finish and 40.5% conditional KO. All-division reference tables prevent isolating only the three motivating men's divisions.

The pathways provide particularly clear target separation: B2 takedown conversion against weak TD defense has 49.1% generic finish (close to 49.9% overall), but 44.8% submission among finishes versus 35.8% overall. B3/B5 have 50.8%/53.7% submission among finishes. Method composition can change even when total finish prevalence changes little.

The completed #116 H1/H2 experiment is retained as evidence about its **specific FINISH-vs-DECISION specification**. This audit does not reverse its unfavorable predictive result, attribute failure to a single cause, or reuse its performance to select categories. Hierarchy versus the earlier baseline also changes regularization and label normalization; historical differences alone do not prove what caused the result.

## What is plausible, and what remains unproven?

Division-specific conditional method baselines make constrained partial pooling **plausible for a future MOV1 experiment**, especially when dividing 2,822 finishes into groups leaves small samples. That is a stronger target-specific rationale than assuming all finish pressure is interchangeable. It is not evidence that hierarchy will outperform regularized fixed intercepts, or that it is universally more suitable for MOV1 than MOV0.

The audit supports preregistering a bounded comparison of simple categorical intercepts and grouped/hierarchical intercepts. It does not determine priors, pooling groups, shrinkage strength or division-specific pathway slopes. Era-level women's and five-round conditional samples, B3 division cells, and Heavyweight B5 are often thin/insufficient. No outcome-driven grouping or new hierarchy is implemented.

Future testing must use training-only group/preprocessing estimates, frozen chronological evaluation, unchanged canonical target and permanent terrain, and direct validation of composed KO/SUB/decision probabilities. **No hierarchical rerun occurred.**
