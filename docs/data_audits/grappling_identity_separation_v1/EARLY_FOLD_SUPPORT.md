Shares use every scoring fighter-side state, including debuts and unavailable dimensions. support.csv separately covers original training dates, January boundary unique training fighters, every division, matched denominators, activity numerators and low-support/missingness. Boundary cohorts must not be mistaken for support at the earlier training fights.

A requires both identities supported for >=50% at every scoring year; stricter decision support and early replication are additional gates.

Histories use strict event-date priors with target and same-date exclusion, sealed through 2026-08-15. Continuous behavioral rates do not establish intention, top control, true ground exposure or causal effects. This is developmental historical evidence; repeated fighters and nested boundaries are dependent. No predictive model or prospective confirmation outcomes were used.
