# Decision-only control identity

CONTROL = control seconds / (60 × jointly observed decision elapsed minutes). Prior DECISION fights only, including decisions regardless of winner. Preserve control_num, control_den_min, control_bouts, control_missing_bouts and prior_decisions. >=1 measured decision and >=15 decision minutes is primary; >=2 decisions is stricter. In block replication, all decisions in each fixed three-bout block must have complete measurement; missing decisions are never replaced by later ones. No prior decision means missing raw control.

Decision conditioning reduces terminal truncation but selects opponent/durability/style environments. High agreement with all-history share does not prove decision-only superiority. Transparent 900-second prior exposure is a descriptive sensitivity; seconds are correlated duration, not independent Bernoulli trials.

Histories use strict event-date priors with target and same-date exclusion, sealed through 2026-08-15. Continuous behavioral rates do not establish intention, top control, true ground exposure or causal effects. This is developmental historical evidence; repeated fighters and nested boundaries are dependent. No predictive model or prospective confirmation outcomes were used.
