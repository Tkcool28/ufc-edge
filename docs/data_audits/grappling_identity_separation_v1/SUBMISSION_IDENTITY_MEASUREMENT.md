# Submission-seeking identity

SUBMISSION = SUB attempts / jointly observed governed elapsed minutes, all historical complete measured bouts. >=3 matched bouts and >=15 minutes is broad support; block replication requires all three bouts measured. Recorded zero attempts with observed exposure is meaningful zero activity; absence of history is missing. No SUB-win outcome term, attempt quality, conversion or grounded denominator is introduced.

The fixed 15-minute prior-exposure sensitivity is within-division, strict-prior and excludes the focal fighter. It is descriptive; it does not turn unsupported states into usable identities.

Histories use strict event-date priors with target and same-date exclusion, sealed through 2026-08-15. Continuous behavioral rates do not establish intention, top control, true ground exposure or causal effects. This is developmental historical evidence; repeated fighters and nested boundaries are dependent. No predictive model or prospective confirmation outcomes were used.
