# Directional matchup readiness

| Pathway | Available sides | Readiness limit |
|---|---|---|
| Control | A decision control/access; B measured control allowed and TD resistance | Generic CTRL includes clinch; no disengagement/escape opportunity or top retention |
| Submission | A SUB activity; B attempts faced/prior SUB exposure | No threat quality, genuine opportunity or calibrated defense trials |
| Ground strikes | A significant ground activity; B significant ground activity allowed | No top/bottom, damage or ground finish location; noisier tendency |

Future actor/opponent construction is conceptually feasible but is not implemented or approved here. Escape/disengagement remains unavailable, never filled with zero or a reversal proxy. A control × B control-allowed is an observed-behavior hypothesis, not a disengagement mechanism. Rates cannot directly become simulator transition hazards.

Histories use strict event-date priors with target and same-date exclusion, sealed through 2026-08-15. Continuous behavioral rates do not establish intention, top control, true ground exposure or causal effects. This is developmental historical evidence; repeated fighters and nested boundaries are dependent. No predictive model or prospective confirmation outcomes were used.
