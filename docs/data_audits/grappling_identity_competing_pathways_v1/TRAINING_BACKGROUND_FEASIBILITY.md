Secondary source feasibility only. No fighter credentials were assigned, no biographies were ingested into numeric tables, and no current credential was used for an old prediction.

Read-only checks performed 2026-10-07 UTC; see EXTERNAL_BACKGROUND_SOURCES.json for URLs, evidence status and limitations. No purchased data, outreach or license conclusion. Public page availability alone does not establish redistribution or modeling rights.

The scientifically defensible future role is a weak uncertain prior for fighters with little governed UFC behavior, based on dated, identity-verified credentials known before the target fight. It must decay in influence as behavioral history accumulates. Wrestling accomplishments do not identify grinder versus GnP preference; a BJJ belt does not establish submission creation/conversion. Gym membership is especially weak and time-varying. No numeric effect, fixed credential weight or automatic label is recommended here.

A future acquisition contract would require achievement date, evidence-publication date or archived contemporaneous record, source/provider ID, canonical fighter ID, credential category and discipline, snapshot/hash, ambiguity/missingness, rights, and negative/unknown distinction. Coverage must be measured without looking at target outcomes. This feasibility review does not establish that such a historically governed credential panel can currently be built at scale.
